FAQ

Common Questions

Do you ship discreetly?

Yes. All orders ship in plain, unmarked packaging with no external indication of the contents. Nothing on the outside references Autofire Genetics or cannabis.

Are all seeds feminized?

Yes — every strain in our catalog is 100% feminized. No males, no guesswork. You grow females from seed, every time.

What growing experience do I need?

Our genetics are selected for vigour and adaptability, making them suitable for both beginner and experienced growers. That said, better technique always yields better results.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes. Use the contact form above to inquire about bulk and wholesale pricing. We work with dispensaries, grow stores, and commercial cultivators.

What if I have a problem with my order?

Contact us directly via the form above or on Instagram. We stand behind every pack we ship and will work with you to make it right.

When are new strains dropping?

New releases are announced on Instagram first. Follow @autofiregenetics and subscribe to our email list to be the first to know about new drops.
