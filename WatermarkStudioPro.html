💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
For social media previews, use a diagonal tile pattern — it is much harder to remove than a single mark.
✓
Set opacity between 20–40% — visible enough to deter theft, subtle enough not to ruin the preview.
✓
Your shop name or website URL makes the most effective watermark text.
✓
Place watermarks over the center of the design, not just corners — corners are easy to crop out.
✓
Use a logo watermark instead of text for a more professional and branded appearance.
✓
Watermark your listing preview images before uploading, but keep unwatermarked copies for buyers.
✓
Never watermark the actual digital product files you deliver to buyers — only your preview images.
