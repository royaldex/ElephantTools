💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Put your primary keyword at the start of your meta title whenever possible.
✓
Keep meta titles under 60 characters to avoid truncation in Google results.
✓
Meta descriptions should be under 160 characters and read like a persuasive one-liner.
✓
Include a call to action in your meta description: 'Learn more', 'Shop now', 'Download free'.
✓
Every page should have a unique meta title and description — duplicates confuse Google.
✓
Use the SERP Preview tool to see exactly how your meta tags look before publishing.
✓
For product pages, include price, free shipping, or other differentiators in the description.
