💡 Pro Tips

✓
Keyword density: your primary keyword should appear 1-2% of total words for good SEO without over-optimisation.
✓
Common stopwords (the, a, and) should be filtered to see meaningful word distribution.
✓
For Etsy listings: check that your primary keyword appears at least 2-3 times in the description.
✓
Writing quality indicator: if one word appears far more than others, you may be over-relying on it.
✓
Compare frequency before and after editing to see if revisions improved vocabulary variety.
✓
For blog posts: check that your target keyword appears in the first 100 words and throughout the content.
✓
Different from character frequency: word frequency shows semantic patterns rather than character distribution.
