💡 Pro Tips

✓
Start your thread with a strong hook — the first tweet determines whether people click 'Show more'.
✓
Number your tweets with (1/N) so readers know how long the thread is and feel invested.
✓
Each tweet should deliver value on its own — a thread is also a series of individual posts.
✓
Use short sentences and line breaks — threads are scanned, not read.
✓
End with a clear call to action: follow, like, retweet, or click the link.
✓
The best thread lengths are 5-15 tweets — under 5 is too short, over 20 loses engagement.
✓
Reply to your own first tweet to continue the thread — do not post standalone tweets mid-thread.
