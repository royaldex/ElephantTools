The Story

obsession.

Our Mission

To prove that autoflower genetics can be connoisseur-grade — full stop.

Autofire Genetics was built by a grower, for growers. The goal has never changed: breed autoflower seeds with the same obsessive attention to potency, terpenes, and consistency that the best photoperiod breeders apply to their work — and make those genetics accessible to anyone dedicated enough to grow them.

I
No Compromises

Every strain in our catalog has been grown out in full. If a line doesn't meet the bar on potency, terpenes, yield, and consistency — it doesn't get released. There is no B-tier at Autofire.

II
Transparency

We tell you the lineage, the expected traits, the terpene profile. No inflated claims, no vague marketing. You know what you're growing before the seed hits soil.

III
Built for Growers

Whether you're running a single tent or a full commercial facility, our genetics are selected to perform across environments. Stability and vigour are non-negotiable selection criteria.

The Process

How a strain earns its place.

01

Source Selection

We start with elite parent genetics — lines with documented performance, proven stability, and traits worth preserving. The foundation matters. We don't build on mediocre stock.

02

Crossing & Backcrossing

Target traits are identified early. The breeding program then focuses multiple generations on amplifying those traits while maintaining the vigour and adaptability that autoflowers require.

03

Phenotype Hunting

Every generation is grown in full. We're looking for the individual plants that express the profile we're chasing — the one that smells the way it should, finishes on schedule, and yields without compromise.

04

Stability Testing

Before release, the line is run multiple times across different environments to confirm consistency. If there's variance we can't account for, it goes back into the breeding program.

05

Catalog Release

Only after passing every benchmark does a strain earn a spot in the catalog. The name, the lineage, the profile — everything is documented and stands behind every pack we ship.
