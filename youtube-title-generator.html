💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
YouTube titles should be under 60 characters to display fully in search results.
✓
Put your primary keyword as close to the start of the title as possible.
✓
Brackets and parentheses at the end of titles add context and improve CTR: [Full Guide] (2025).
✓
Your thumbnail and title work together — they must tell a consistent story.
✓
The first 150 characters of your description appear in search results — make them count.
✓
Add timestamps to your description — YouTube shows them in search results and improves watch time.
✓
Include your primary keyword in the first 50 words of your description for SEO.
