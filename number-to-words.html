💡 Pro Tips

✓
Currency mode adds 'dollars and cents only' — the standard format for cheques and legal invoices.
✓
For invoices: always write the amount in words AND numerals to prevent fraud or disputes.
✓
Ordinal mode converts 1 to 'first', 2 to 'second' — useful for competition results and rankings.
✓
Numbers above 999 trillion will be truncated — these exceed everyday practical use cases.
✓
Year mode uses the traditional spoken form: 1984 becomes 'nineteen eighty-four'.
✓
For legal documents, always have the amount reviewed — spelling errors in legal amounts can cause disputes.
✓
Use this for accessibility: screen readers handle written numbers better than raw digits in some contexts.
