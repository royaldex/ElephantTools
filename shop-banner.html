💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Etsy recommends a minimum banner size of 3360 x 840px for the big banner format.
✓
Keep text large and minimal — your banner will be viewed at many different screen sizes.
✓
Use your brand colors consistently across your banner, logo, and listing images.
✓
Seasonal banners drive engagement — update your banner for major holidays and sales events.
✓
Include your unique selling proposition in the tagline: 'Instant Download' or 'Commercial Use OK'.
✓
A solid color background with your logo and shop name often outperforms busy image backgrounds.
✓
Test your banner on both desktop and mobile — the mobile crop is much smaller and centered.
