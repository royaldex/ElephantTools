Wall Art Tips
Always use 300 DPI for print — it ensures crisp output at any listed frame size
Etsy's 20MB per-file limit applies — this tool targets just under 20MB automatically
Use Fit mode with a white or cream background for consistent gallery-style padding
For POD / Printify / Printful, use 300 DPI JPEG — they prefer it over PNG
Portrait orientation is the best seller — most wall art buyers hang vertically
Include all sizes in one ZIP using the Wall Art Resizer for multi-size bundles
