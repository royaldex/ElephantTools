💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Always design at the largest size first, then resize down — never upscale from a small source file.
✓
The most popular wall art sizes on Etsy are 8x10, 5x7, and 11x14 — always include these.
✓
Design at 300 DPI from the start. This means an 18x24 print needs a 5400x7200 pixel canvas.
✓
Offer multiple size bundles in one listing — buyers love the convenience of getting all sizes at once.
✓
Check proportions before resizing — some standard sizes are different ratios and may require padding.
✓
Use the Wall Art Resizer (basic version) for single-image resizing with standard presets.
✓
Include a 'size guide' image in your listing showing each file size next to common objects for scale.
