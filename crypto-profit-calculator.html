💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Always include exchange fees in your calculations — they can significantly impact your real profit.
✓
Track every trade in a spreadsheet or app — gains and losses are taxable events in most countries.
✓
ROI (return on investment) tells you the percentage gain or loss relative to your starting capital.
✓
Use the portfolio calculator to track multiple positions at once for a complete picture.
✓
Consider the cost of converting back to fiat (withdrawal fees, network fees) in your calculations.
✓
Fees compound across multiple trades — frequent trading in smaller amounts is often less profitable.
✓
This calculator is for educational purposes — always consult a tax professional for crypto tax advice.
