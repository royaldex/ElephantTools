💡 Pro Tips

✓
The hook must happen in the first 3 seconds — state the problem or the payoff immediately.
✓
Viewer retention drops sharply at 30 seconds — re-hook the audience again at this point.
✓
Write your script in spoken language — read it aloud and edit anything that sounds unnatural.
✓
Include B-roll cues in your script so you know what visuals to capture or source.
✓
The CTA should be mentioned twice — once mid-video and once at the end.
✓
Pattern interrupts (questions, cuts, graphics) every 60-90 seconds maintain attention in longer videos.
✓
For TikTok: spend 50% of your prep time on the hook — it determines everything.
