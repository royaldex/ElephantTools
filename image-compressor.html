💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
For product listing photos: target under 1MB per image for fast page loading.
✓
JPEG quality of 85% is visually indistinguishable from 100% for most images at normal viewing distance.
✓
PNG files with large solid-color areas compress very well — try them at quality 90%.
✓
WebP format produces 25–35% smaller files than JPEG at the same visual quality.
✓
Always do a final visual check by zooming into details — look for blurring or color banding.
✓
For Etsy digital downloads, compress your listing preview images but deliver full-resolution product files.
✓
Compressing before upload speeds up your Etsy shop for buyers on mobile connections.
