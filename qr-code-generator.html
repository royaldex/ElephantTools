💡 Pro Tips

✓
QR codes work best at 300px or larger for physical printing — use 600px for business cards and flyers.
✓
Always test your QR code by scanning it before printing or distributing it.
✓
Keep URLs short — shorter content means denser, more scannable QR codes.
✓
For business use, link to a URL redirector so you can update the destination without reprinting.
✓
White QR codes on dark backgrounds work — just make sure contrast is at least 4:1.
✓
QR codes with error correction can survive up to 30% damage — useful for codes on textured surfaces.
✓
For Etsy: add a QR code to your packaging linking buyers to your shop for repeat purchases.
