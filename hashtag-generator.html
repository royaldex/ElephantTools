💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
On Instagram, mix 5–10 high-volume tags (1M+ posts) with 10–15 niche tags (under 100K posts).
✓
TikTok hashtags work differently — fewer is better. Use 3–5 highly relevant tags plus #fyp.
✓
Pinterest hashtags are keyword-style — match exactly what people search for on the platform.
✓
Avoid banned or overused hashtags — Instagram penalizes accounts that use them repeatedly.
✓
Research competitor posts in your niche to find hashtags that are working right now.
✓
Rotate your hashtag sets across posts — using the same 30 tags every time can reduce reach.
✓
Save your best-performing hashtag sets in notes so you can reuse them efficiently.
