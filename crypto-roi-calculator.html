💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Annualized ROI lets you compare investments held for different time periods on equal footing.
✓
A 2x return (100% ROI) over 4 years is a lower annualized return than a 2x return in 1 year.
✓
Investment multiple tells you how many times your money has grown: 2x = doubled, 5x = quintupled.
✓
Always calculate ROI after fees — exchange fees and withdrawal costs reduce your real return.
✓
Compare crypto ROI against the same period's stock market performance for context.
✓
Past ROI does not predict future performance — crypto markets are highly volatile.
✓
This calculator is for educational purposes — always consult a financial advisor before investing.
