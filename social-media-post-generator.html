💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Never post the exact same text on every platform — audiences and algorithms differ significantly.
✓
LinkedIn posts perform best with professional storytelling and personal insights.
✓
Twitter/X rewards brevity and strong opinions — get to the point in the first sentence.
✓
Facebook posts with questions get the most comments — end with a direct question.
✓
Instagram captions should be longer and more conversational than other platforms.
✓
Schedule posts in advance using your platform's scheduler or a tool like Buffer.
✓
Post consistently — 3–5 times per week on Instagram and daily on Twitter for best results.
