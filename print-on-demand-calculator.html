💡 Pro Tips

✓
Aim for at least 30% profit margin — anything under 20% makes scaling mathematically difficult.
✓
Printify Premium ($29/month) pays for itself at roughly 15-20 orders per month for most products.
✓
Include free shipping in your price instead of charging separately — listings with free shipping rank higher on Etsy.
✓
Etsy charges 6.5% on the TOTAL buyer payment — including whatever shipping you charge.
✓
Offsite Ads (15% fee) are mandatory if your shop earns over $10,000/year — factor this into your pricing.
✓
Set your price to hit 30% margin even WITH Offsite Ads — that way you profit regardless.
✓
Gelato often has better international shipping rates — compare base costs for your specific product types.
