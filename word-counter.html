💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Etsy titles max out at 140 characters — use Word Counter to check before switching to Title Optimizer.
✓
Etsy tags max out at 20 characters each — paste individual tags to verify quickly.
✓
Meta descriptions should be under 160 characters for Google to display them without truncation.
✓
For blog posts, aim for 1500–2500 words to hit the sweet spot for SEO and reader engagement.
✓
Average reading speed is 200–250 words per minute — use reading time to plan content length.
✓
Twitter/X enforces a 280-character limit — use Word Counter to draft and refine your tweets.
✓
LinkedIn posts perform best between 150–300 words — beyond that engagement typically drops.
