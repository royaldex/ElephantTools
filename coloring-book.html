💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Images with strong contrast and clear edges produce the best coloring page outlines.
✓
Simple illustrations and cartoons convert better than complex photographs.
✓
Try all four algorithms and compare — the best one varies by image type.
✓
Coloring pages are a high-demand Etsy digital product — they sell well year-round.
✓
Create themed coloring books: animals, holidays, mandalas, affirmations — niche sells better.
✓
After generating, clean up the lines in a drawing app like Procreate or Adobe Fresco.
✓
Price individual pages at $1–3 and full coloring books at $5–15 for best conversion.
