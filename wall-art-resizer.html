💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Design your source file at the largest size you sell, then resize down — never upscale from small.
✓
A 300 DPI standard means an 8x10 print needs 2400 x 3000 pixels minimum.
✓
Offering multiple sizes in one Etsy listing is a powerful way to increase the perceived value.
✓
US letter sizes (8.5x11) and A4 are popular international print sizes — include both.
✓
The most popular wall art sizes sold on Etsy are 8x10, 5x7, 11x14, and 16x20.
✓
Always tell buyers which sizes are included in your product description.
✓
Use Wall Art Sizer Pro for batch resizing multiple sizes simultaneously.
