💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
300 DPI is the professional print standard. At 300 DPI, a 3000px image prints at 10 inches.
✓
For Etsy wall art, create files at 300 DPI at the largest size you sell (e.g. 18x24 inches = 5400x7200px).
✓
Print-on-demand platforms like Printify have specific minimum resolution requirements — check them before listing.
✓
A 72 DPI screen-resolution image will look blurry when printed, even if it looks good on screen.
✓
If your image is too small, use PNG Upscaler Pro to enlarge it — but start with the highest quality source you have.
✓
Checking resolution before listing saves you from bad reviews about blurry print quality.
✓
Canva exports at 96 DPI by default. Always export at 300 DPI for print products.
