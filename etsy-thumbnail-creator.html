💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
White or very light backgrounds dramatically improve click-through rate in Etsy search results.
✓
Use a consistent thumbnail style across all listings — it makes your shop look professional.
✓
Etsy recommends a 2000 x 2000px minimum — always check your output resolution.
✓
Avoid text-heavy thumbnails — they become illegible in small grid view on mobile.
✓
Show the product clearly on a clean background. Buyers need to understand it at a glance.
✓
Create multiple thumbnail styles and test them with Etsy's duplicate listing feature.
✓
Keep your main product centered with generous padding on all sides.
