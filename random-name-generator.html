💡 Pro Tips

✓
For character names: pick a name that fits the character's personality — harsh consonants feel strong, soft vowels feel gentle.
✓
Business names work best when they are short (1-2 words), memorable, and available as a .com domain.
✓
Product names should hint at the benefit or feeling without being too literal — 'Slack' is not about rope.
✓
Check name availability on Namechk.com before committing — you want consistent handles across all platforms.
✓
Fantasy names feel authentic when they follow consistent phonetic rules — create internal logic for your world.
✓
For Etsy shop names: the generated name still needs to pass the 4-20 character, letters-only rule.
✓
Use multiple regenerations and save your favourites — the best names often appear after a few attempts.
