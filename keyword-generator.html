💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Long-tail keywords (3+ words) have less competition and higher buyer intent than single keywords.
✓
Use question-style keywords for blog content — they often rank in Google's featured snippets.
✓
Etsy keywords should reflect what buyers search for, not how you describe your product.
✓
Seasonal modifiers dramatically boost traffic at the right time: 'Christmas gift', 'Valentine's Day'.
✓
Use this tool to find title and tag ideas, then validate with Etsy's search bar autocomplete.
✓
Export keyword lists to a spreadsheet and track which ones appear in your Etsy Stats traffic.
✓
Refresh your keyword research every 3–6 months — search trends shift over time.
