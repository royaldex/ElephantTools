💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
WebP is supported by all major browsers — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge all render it natively.
✓
For Etsy shops: WebP reduces your shop's visual load time, improving mobile buyer experience.
✓
Quality 85% WebP typically looks identical to quality 100% JPEG at roughly half the file size.
✓
WebP also supports transparency like PNG but at much smaller sizes — ideal for logos and art.
✓
Not all platforms accept WebP uploads — check before converting files meant for Etsy downloads.
✓
Always keep your original JPEG/PNG source files as WebP is not universally accepted everywhere.
✓
Use WebP for website assets and social media previews — stick to PNG/JPG for product files.
