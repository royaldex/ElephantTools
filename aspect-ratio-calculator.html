💡 Pro Tips

✓
16:9 is the universal widescreen standard — use it for YouTube thumbnails (1280x720px minimum).
✓
Instagram feed posts perform best at 4:5 portrait — it takes up more screen real estate in the feed.
✓
Etsy listing images default to a square grid — always keep key subject matter centered.
✓
For wall art prints: 2:3 ratio (e.g. 4x6, 8x12, 16x24) is the most popular selling format on Etsy.
✓
TikTok and Instagram Reels require 9:16 — design at 1080x1920px for full quality.
✓
Avoid letterboxing or pillarboxing in videos — crop to the native platform ratio instead.
✓
Pinterest pins perform best at 2:3 ratio (1000x1500px) — the tall format dominates feeds.
