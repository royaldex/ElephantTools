How This Works
Uses binary search to find the exact quality/scale that fits under your target size
JPG gives the smallest file size — best for photos and detailed wall art
PNG is lossless compression — file sizes will be larger than JPG
WEBP is the most efficient — but check your buyer's software supports it
For print products, JPG at 90%+ quality at 300 DPI is visually indistinguishable from raw

💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
JPEG compression is lossy — stay above 80% quality to avoid visible artifacts.
✓
PNG files with transparency are better converted to WebP for smaller size.
✓
For listing photos (not the digital product itself), aim for under 5MB for fast page loads.
✓
Always check your compressed file visually before uploading — zoom in to check quality.
✓
For print products, keep working files at full resolution and only compress listing photos.
✓
Batch compress multiple images at once using the Image Compressor tool for efficiency.
✓
Keep your original high-resolution files backed up — only upload the compressed versions.
