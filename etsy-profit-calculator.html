💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Etsy charges 6.5% on the total amount the buyer pays — including shipping. Factor this in.
✓
Offsite Ads (15%) only apply when a buyer clicks an Etsy ad. You cannot opt out if you earn over $10k/yr.
✓
Payment processing (3% + $0.25) is charged on every transaction — don't forget the flat $0.25.
✓
Use the reverse calculator to find exactly what price you need to charge to hit a target profit.
✓
For Printify sellers: the $29/month Premium plan often pays for itself at 20+ orders per month.
✓
Aim for at least a 30% profit margin — anything under 20% makes scaling unsustainable.
✓
Factor in your time. Even free tools have a cost — your hours are worth money.
