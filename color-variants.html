💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Offering color variants significantly increases your chances of a sale — buyers have different preferences.
✓
Create a set of 5–10 color variants from a single design to multiply your catalog instantly.
✓
List each color variant as a separate Etsy listing, or offer them as a bundle in a single listing.
✓
Neutral color variants (black, white, navy) tend to be the best sellers regardless of product type.
✓
Seasonal color variants are a great way to refresh existing designs — a Halloween orange or Christmas red version.
✓
Keep your original design file — variants are generated from it and you may need to re-export.
✓
Match your color variants to Pantone or brand color references if selling to commercial clients.
