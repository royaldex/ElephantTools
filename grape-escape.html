About This Strain

Grape Escape is an 80/20 indica-dominant true F1 hybrid — El Chemi Kiwi from Mephisto Genetics crossed with a reversed Cosmic Cupcake from Supernatural Seeds. The result is one of the most distinct terpene profiles we've put out.

From the first dry hit to the exhale, a tsunami of heavy grape terpenes washes over the palate — reminiscent of grape SweeTARTS candy. Sweet, tangy, and intensely flavorful. The effect matches the profile: a powerfully indica-like cerebral experience with a sensation of pressure behind the eyebrows that settles into deep, comfortable relaxation. This is a strain for those who enjoy a potent, heavy head high.
