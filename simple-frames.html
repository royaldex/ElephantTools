💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
White borders with a thin inner shadow give a gallery-quality feel to wall art previews.
✓
Match your frame color to your brand palette for a consistent shop aesthetic.
✓
Thin, elegant borders (1–3% of image width) work better for art — thick borders distract.
✓
Use frames consistently across all listing images to make your shop look curated.
✓
A subtle off-white or cream border often photographs better than pure white on product mockups.
✓
For digital art selling as prints, show one framed version in your listing to help buyers visualize the product.
✓
Avoid colored frames on colorful artwork — the frame should complement, not compete.
