💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Update current prices regularly — crypto prices change by the minute.
✓
Use your average buy price if you have made multiple purchases of the same asset at different prices.
✓
The allocation chart shows what percentage of your portfolio each coin represents — diversify intentionally.
✓
A well-diversified portfolio limits the damage any single coin's crash can do to your total value.
✓
Record your initial investments carefully — accurate buy prices are essential for correct P&L tracking.
✓
Track unrealized P&L here, but realize that gains are only real when you sell.
✓
This tool is for personal tracking only — not financial advice or tax accounting.
