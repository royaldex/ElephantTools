💡 Pro Tips

✓
A favicon should be readable at 16x16 pixels — avoid complex logos with fine text at this scale.
✓
Use a single bold letter, logo mark, or emoji as your favicon — simpler is always better at small sizes.
✓
For iOS home screen (Apple Touch Icon), provide the 180x180 PNG with no rounded corners — iOS rounds them automatically.
✓
For Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), the 512x512 icon is required in your web app manifest.
✓
Place favicon files in your website root directory so browsers find them automatically at /favicon.ico.
✓
A transparent PNG favicon works in most modern browsers — use a white or brand-colored background for consistency.
✓
Test your favicon in both light and dark browser themes — some favicons look great in one but disappear in the other.
