💡 Pro Tips

✓
The standard Pomodoro is 25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break.
✓
Eliminate all distractions before starting — phone on silent, browser tabs closed, notifications off.
✓
After 4 Pomodoros, take a longer break (15-30 minutes) to fully recover your focus.
✓
Adjust session length to suit your work: deep coding may need 50-minute blocks, writing may need 25.
✓
Use the break timer as a strict deadline — do not extend breaks, it defeats the purpose.
✓
Keep a notepad nearby during sessions — write down distracting thoughts and return to them later.
✓
Track completed Pomodoros to understand your productive hours and daily capacity.
