Etsy Title Tips
Put your most important keywords in the first 40 characters
Use commas or pipes to separate keyword phrases
Avoid repeating words — Etsy ignores duplicates
Include material, style, occasion, and use case
Don't waste characters on "and" or "the" between keywords

💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

✓
Front-load your primary keyword — Etsy weights the beginning of your title most heavily in search.
✓
Use all 140 characters whenever possible. Unused characters are missed SEO opportunities.
✓
Separate keyword phrases with commas or pipe characters (|) for better readability.
✓
Avoid filler words like 'a', 'the', 'and' at the start — put a keyword there instead.
✓
Include your file format (PNG, SVG, PDF) if selling digital products — buyers search for this.
✓
Research what terms buyers actually use. Think like a customer, not a creator.
✓
Update your titles seasonally — add 'Christmas Gift' or 'Birthday Present' when relevant.
