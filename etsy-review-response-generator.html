💡 Pro Tips

✓
Responding to every review — positive and negative — signals to Etsy's algorithm that you are an engaged seller.
✓
For negative reviews, never argue or make excuses publicly. Acknowledge, apologise, and offer a solution.
✓
Keep responses under 200 words — longer responses can feel defensive rather than genuine.
✓
Thank the buyer by first name if they have left one — personal responses feel more authentic.
✓
A genuine, warm response to a 5-star review can prompt that buyer to share your shop with friends.
✓
Responding to negative reviews publicly shows potential buyers how you handle problems — it builds trust.
✓
Do not copy-paste identical responses — vary your wording so your shop does not look automated.
