💡 Pro Tips

✓
Etsy shop names must be 4-20 characters, no spaces, no special characters except letters and numbers.
✓
Shorter names are easier to remember, easier to type, and look cleaner in URLs and on packaging.
✓
Check your shortlisted names on Etsy's shop name field before getting attached — many good ones are taken.
✓
Avoid numbers and hyphens — they make names harder to communicate verbally and harder to find.
✓
A good shop name reflects what you sell OR creates a brand feeling. Ideally both.
✓
Search the name on Google and social media too — you want consistent availability across platforms.
✓
Your shop name is also your Etsy URL — make sure it is something you are happy sharing everywhere.
