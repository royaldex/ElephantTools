💡 Pro Tips

✓
BMI is a screening tool only — it does not account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition.
✓
Athletes and muscular individuals often have high BMI despite having low body fat.
✓
BMI is less accurate for people over 65 or under 18 — different guidelines apply.
✓
A BMI of 18.5-24.9 is considered healthy for most adults, but context matters significantly.
✓
Waist circumference is often a better predictor of health risk than BMI for adults.
✓
Always consult a healthcare professional for personalised health and weight guidance.
✓
BMI should be one of many data points — not the sole measure of health or fitness.
